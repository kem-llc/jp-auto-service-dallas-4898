Extend tire life and improve handling with our tire services.

Overview

Proper tire maintenance ensures safety and efficiency. We offer tire rotation, balancing, mounting, and wheel alignment to promote even wear, better fuel economy, and a smoother ride.

What's included

  • Even tire wear for longer life
  • Better fuel efficiency
  • Improved handling and safety
  • Reduced road noise

How it works

1

Tire Inspection

We check tread depth, pressure, and overall condition.

2

Rotation & Balancing

Tires are rotated and balanced to prevent uneven wear.

3

Alignment Check

We adjust angles to manufacturer specs for optimal handling.

Common questions

How often should I rotate my tires?

Every 5,000-7,500 miles, typically during an oil change.

Do I need an alignment if my car pulls to one side?

Yes, that's a common sign of misalignment. We can check and correct it.
