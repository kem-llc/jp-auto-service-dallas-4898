Our story

Your Trusted Local Auto Repair Shop in Dallas

To provide honest, high-quality auto repair services that keep our community safe on the road.

JP Auto Service was founded with a simple mission: deliver expert auto repair with integrity. Our founder, a lifelong mechanic, saw too many customers overcharged for unnecessary work. He built JP Auto on transparency, fair pricing, and technical excellence. Today, we serve Dallas drivers from our Rowan Avenue shop, treating every car as if it were our own.

Over the years, we've grown from a small two-bay garage to a full-service shop equipped with the latest diagnostic tools. But our core values remain unchanged. We invest in our team's training, use quality parts, and take the time to educate our customers. Whether it's a simple oil change or a complex engine repair, you can count on JP Auto Service for reliable, honest work.
