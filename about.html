Our story

Your Trusted Dallas Auto Repair Shop

To provide honest, high-quality automotive service that keeps our community safe and mobile, with transparency and integrity at every step.

JP Auto Service was founded in 2008 by John P., a master technician with over 20 years of experience. Starting as a small two-bay shop on Rowan Avenue, John built a reputation for meticulous work and fair pricing. Word spread quickly among Dallas residents who were tired of high-pressure sales and shoddy repairs.

Over the years, we've grown into a full-service auto repair facility serving the entire Dallas metro area. Our team of ASE-certified mechanics shares John's commitment to excellence. We invest in the latest diagnostic equipment and ongoing training to handle everything from vintage cars to the latest hybrids. At JP Auto Service, you're not just a customer—you're a neighbor.
